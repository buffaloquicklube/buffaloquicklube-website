Semi Synthetic Vs. Full Synthetic Oil

Which one does your vehicle need?

Semi synthetic and full synthetic oils are both designed to protect your engine, but they are not the same. The right choice usually depends on your vehicle, mileage, manufacturer recommendation, driving habits, and budget.

Semi synthetic oil blends conventional and synthetic base oils. It is a common choice for many vehicles and can be a good balance of protection and value.

Full synthetic oil is built for stronger performance in heat, cold, and demanding driving conditions. Many newer vehicles require full synthetic oil, and it can help support longer-lasting engine protection when matched to the correct service interval.

The best answer is not always the most expensive option. At Buffalo Quick Lube, we can help explain what your vehicle calls for and what options make sense before service begins.

Quick Takeaway

If you are unsure which oil type your vehicle needs, ask us when you stop in. We will look at the vehicle requirements and explain your options clearly.
