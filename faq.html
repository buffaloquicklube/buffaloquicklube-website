FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers about appointments, service time, location, and pricing.

Do I need an appointment?

Nope, not for oil changes. Oil changes are drive-up service only. Interior details do require an appointment.

How long does an oil change usually take?

The typical wait time is about 10 to 15 minutes, depending on traffic and vehicle needs.

Where are you located?

We are at 300 10th Ave S, Buffalo, MN 55313, behind Speedway off Hwy 55 and attached to Tires Plus.

What types of oil do you stock?

We regularly stock these common oil options:

  • 5W20 Semi Synthetic Blend
  • 5W30 Semi Synthetic Blend
  • 5W30 Full Synthetic Dexos
  • 0W20 Full Synthetic Dexos
  • 10W30 Synthetic Blend
  • 0W30 Full Synthetic
  • 0W16 Full Synthetic
  • 0W40 Full Synthetic

If your vehicle needs an oil type we do not normally carry, call or message us ahead of time and we can order in what you need.

Why can oil change pricing vary?

Pricing can change based on oil type, oil quantity, filter type, and vehicle requirements.

Do you replace wipers, bulbs, filters, and batteries?

Yes. Ask the team for availability and pricing for your vehicle.

Do you rotate tires?

No, we do not perform tire rotations. While your oil change is being completed, you are welcome to stop by Tires Plus next door to ask about scheduling tire service.
